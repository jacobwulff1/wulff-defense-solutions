Our Story

A neighborhood landmark
for honest, great food.

Tucked into the thriving Historic-Walk of downtown Tehachapi, Jake's Steakhouse & Seafood has long been a gathering place for quality meals and good company — the kind of room where a celebration dinner and a casual night at the bar feel equally at home.

Every prime rib and steak dinner is cut from Harris Ranch beef, sourced locally through The Butcher Shop in Old Town. The seafood is wild-caught, the pours are generous, and the welcome is genuine.
